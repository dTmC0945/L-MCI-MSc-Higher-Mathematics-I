Linear Algebra I - Fundamentals

Linear Algebra is an extensive subject covering wide topics such as:

vectors, matrices, determinants, systems of linear equations, vector spaces, linear transformations, eigenvalue problems,

and much more. [1] As there are numerous textbooks and publications dedicated to this topic. As an area of study it has a broad appeal in that it has many applications in engineering, physics, geometry, computer science, economics, and other areas. [2] It also contributes to a deeper understanding of mathematics itself.

Matrices, which are rectangular arrays of numbers or functions, and vectors are the main tools of linear algebra. Matrices are important as they allow us express large amounts of data and functions in an organized and concise form. In addition, as matrices are single objects, we denote them by single letters and calculate with them directly.

All these features have made matrices and vectors very popular for expressing scientific and mathematical ideas.
